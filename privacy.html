Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

Overview

Weekly. is a personal task tracker. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we access and how it is used.

Data We Access

When you sign in with Google, Weekly. requests access to Google Sheets and Google Drive solely to create and manage a spreadsheet in your own Google Drive to store your tasks. We do not access any other files in your Drive.

Data Storage

Your tasks are stored in a Google Sheet inside your personal Google Drive account. We do not store your task data on our servers. Your data belongs to you and stays in your Drive.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third party. AI features are processed through an intermediary server that does not log or store your data.

Data Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by deleting the "Weekly Tracker Task Database" spreadsheet from your Google Drive.
